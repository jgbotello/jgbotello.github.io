Profile

My research spans generative AI, machine learning, deep learning, and advanced statistical methods, with applications in modeling and simulation of complex social phenomena. I develop reproducible computational workflows that support AI-assisted analysis and decision-making.

I have collaborated with interdisciplinary research teams in Colombia, the United States, Greece, Turkey, and France, and have teaching and applied experience in industrial and government settings. I work in English and Spanish.
