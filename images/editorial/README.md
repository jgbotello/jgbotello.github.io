# Editorial assets

`research-landscape.png` was created with the built-in image generation tool for
this website. It is decorative artwork and does not depict a claimed fieldwork
location. It is used with empty alt text because the masthead content is real HTML.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: decorative panoramic banner for a refined academic personal website. Generate a very wide landscape photograph-like image of layers of misty forested mountains, serene editorial photography. Pale warm ivory sky and fine atmospheric mist fill the left 55 percent of the composition, leaving almost empty pale space for website text. The right half contains muted sage and deep forest green conifers, distant slate-gray mountain peaks, soft natural morning light. Composition similar to elegant scientific journal landscape photography, understated, believable natural textures, no dramatic saturated colors, no buildings, no people, no text, no watermark, no UI. Wide panoramic 3:1 image, mountains mostly in right third and lower third. This is illustrative decorative artwork, not a specific real location.

`model.svg`, `archive.svg`, `retrieval.svg`, and `access.svg` are small code-native
conceptual diagrams. They illustrate research themes decoratively; they are not
scientific figures or experimental results from the corresponding publications.
