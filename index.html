Research projects

01 / Artificial intelligence and M&S

M&S 2.0: The next generation of modeling and simulation.

This project aims to advance AI methods and develop tools to make simulation models easier to build, retrieve, and reuse. It focuses on rapid simulation development, composability, and interoperability.

02 / Digital libraries

Bringing editorial cartoons back to life.

This project aims to develop an agentic AI framework to make editorial cartoons easier to understand for teaching and historical analysis. It connects cartoons with the events, people, and cultural references behind them.

03 / Social systems

People, place,
and movement.

Studying historical human mobility and how host communities respond to migration.

This paper investigates how large language models (LLMs) can support the transformation of narrative descriptions into system archetypes.
